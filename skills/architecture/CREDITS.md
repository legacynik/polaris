# Credits

The rules in this skill are our own rewording of ideas from the sources below. No text is quoted
from them, and nothing in `SKILL.md`, `references/` or `scripts/` points to, quotes or loads them.

## Books and articles

- *Software Architecture: The Hard Parts* — Neal Ford, Mark Richards, Pramod Sadalage, Zhamak Dehghani → `references/decision-rules/decomposition.md`
- *Building Evolutionary Architectures*, 2nd ed. — Neal Ford, Rebecca Parsons, Patrick Kua, Pramod Sadalage → `references/decision-rules/governance.md`
- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, 1st ed. — Martin Kleppmann → `references/decision-rules/data-systems.md`
- *Team Topologies* — Matthew Skelton, Manuel Pais → `references/decision-rules/ownership.md`
- *Learning Domain-Driven Design* — Vlad Khononov → `references/decision-rules/domain-strategy.md`
- *Clean Architecture* — Robert C. Martin → `references/decision-rules/component-boundary.md` and the four costs in `references/audit-arm.md`
- *A Philosophy of Software Design* — John Ousterhout → depth and the divergent-design premise in `references/interface-and-seam.md` and `references/design-verb.md`
- *Working Effectively with Legacy Code* — Michael Feathers → the seam, in `references/interface-and-seam.md`
- "ARCHITECTURE.md" (article) — Aleksey Kladov → the document contract in `references/doc-arm.md`
