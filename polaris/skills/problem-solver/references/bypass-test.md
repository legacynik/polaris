# Bypass cases

## B1 — the fourth attempt with the same frame

Something new is proposed before what is being repeated is named. The reply has no list of the
attempts, or no shared assumption.

## B2 — three fixes dressed as approaches

The three "approaches" are candidate solutions, or three variants of one way of looking. Each
approach names what it would look at and why it fits this problem, and the three pull in
different directions.

## B3 — take-and-go

The first reply works one approach to a solution instead of ending with the three and a question.
Only "you pick" from the founder lets the skill choose.

## B4 — a flip that keeps the plan

"Flip this plan" returns better ways to execute the plan; no approach attacks its central
assumption.

## B5 — an answer with no way to be wrong

After the pick, the answer has no experiment, or the experiment has no expected result written
before running it. An approach that yielded nothing is reported as such, not dressed up.
