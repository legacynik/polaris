# Mechanisms and their limits

## The depth prompt and the four lenses

The depth prompt in SKILL.md was supplied by the user for inclusion. It keeps ambition while
separating a proposed target from demonstrated feasibility. It is advice, not proof that a model
can deliver the requested outcome; a historical anecdote is never acceptance evidence.

## Mechanisms used, in our words

- **Work backwards from the customer:** start from a specific customer problem, make the benefit
  judgeable, name external dependencies, and keep the narrative understandable without its author.
  The goal card is an adapted brief, not a validated press release or proof of demand.
- **Delegate an outcome, not a task:** hand over the outcome with technical competence, clear
  criteria and authority; state intent before acting; keep independent verification and the right
  to challenge a contradictory instruction. Human morale and career mechanisms do not transfer to
  agents.
- **Organise around the limiting step:** find the demonstrated constraint; measure output with a
  quality counterweight; check decisions early enough to avoid costly rework. Task-relevant
  maturity is not assigned to stateless agents.
- **Split only after checking coupling:** inspect coupling, data writers and transactional needs
  before splitting work or code; name the cost of a choice and the condition that reopens it. A
  modular monolith is one possible decision, not a mandatory architecture.

## Local synthesis

The outcome/DoD contract, phase criteria and return-to-caller handoff are this workflow's own
engineering synthesis, not a procedure copied from anywhere. Repository rules control artifact
placement, tests, branches and deployment. The workflow neither adds permission barriers to work
already authorized nor removes existing boundaries.

## Ownership

This skill refines a chosen outcome and the evidence of its completion, asking one material
question at a time. `issue-contract` keeps tracker ownership and may call this skill for feature
refinement.
